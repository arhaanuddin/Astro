About Astronet

Learn about our society, mission, and the team behind Astronet

Our Mission

To inspire curiosity about the universe and make astronomy accessible to everyone in our community.

Founded in 2014, Astronet Society has grown from a small group of passionate stargazers to a thriving community of over 500 members. We believe that the wonder of the cosmos should be shared and experienced by all, regardless of their background or expertise level.

Through regular observation nights, educational workshops, and community outreach programs, we aim to ignite the spark of curiosity about space in people of all ages.

Our Journey

Key milestones in Astronet's history

2014

Society Founded

Astronet was founded by a group of 12 astronomy enthusiasts at the university.

2016

First Observatory

Acquired our first professional telescope and established the rooftop observatory.

2018

100 Members Milestone

Reached 100 active members and launched our educational workshop program.

2020

Online Expansion

Launched virtual events and online learning resources during the pandemic.

2022

Community Award

Received the Excellence in Science Outreach award from the city council.

2024

10 Years & Growing

Celebrating 10 years with 500+ members and expanded facilities.
